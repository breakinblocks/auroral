# Auroral 1.1.2

## Added

- **Shimmerweave Skates now protect you from falling into lava.** While you are falling or flying downward, any lava directly beneath you turns into obsidian before you reach it.
- A new `skates_lava_fall_scan_depth` config controls how far below you the skates look (default 16 blocks, 0 disables). (breakinblocks/auroral#17)

## Fixed

- Worn Shimmerweave armor no longer shows the missing texture checkerboard. Thanks to smcnultyos. (breakinblocks/auroral#18)
