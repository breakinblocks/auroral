# Auroral 1.1.2

## Added

- **Shimmerweave Skates now protect you from falling into lava.** While you are falling or flying downward, any lava directly beneath you turns into obsidian before you reach it.
- A new `skates_lava_fall_scan_depth` config controls how far below you the skates look (default 16 blocks, 0 disables). (breakinblocks/auroral#17)

- **Shimmer Spear.** A long-reach piercing weapon crafted from a Shimmersteel Ingot and two sticks. Left-click stabs hit every enemy along the line, only at full attack charge. Hold right-click to charge a committed strike that lunges forward and hits harder the longer it is held. Every hit applies Frostbite; during an aurora it deals bonus damage and a stronger, longer Frostbite.
- **Shimmersteel Nautilus Armor.** Crafted from Shimmersteel Ingots around a Nautilus Shell. Right-click a tamed Auroral Nautilus to equip it, shear the nautilus to take it off. It drops when the nautilus dies.
- **Snow-logged Aurora Blooms.** Blooms placed on a snow layer or in powder snow, by you or by the aurora, now replace the snow at the same level and remember it. When the bloom is broken or wilts the snow comes back. Snow-logged blooms keep a visible snow base and give off faint snowflakes so they can be found under fresh snow.
- Aurora Blooms can grow on Ice, Packed Ice and Blue Ice. Silk touching a fully grown bloom now gives the decorative variant. The decorative bloom can be placed in flower pots.
- Shimmerweave Goggles now show a translucent visor over the eyes.
- Lit Hearthwood Logs ignite phantoms that enter their area and phantoms will not target players standing in it.
- Tamed Auroral Nautili within 32 blocks follow you through portals and teleports. Tamed nautili orbit closer, wild ones orbit a player holding an Aurora Shard, and at most two spawn within 64 blocks of any player.
- Glacial Basins take items from pipes and hoppers into an input slot, infuse them automatically, and push results out the bottom. Sneak and right-click with an empty hand to empty the output slot.
- Shimmersteel Ingots work as an armor trim material. Shimmersteel tools and Shimmerweave armor accept the vanilla enchantments for their type.
- Shimmer Soil accepts vanilla crop seeds.
- Shimmersteel Bow damage scales with draw strength, respects Power and Infinity, and the Star-Shot counts as an arrow.
- Shimmersteel Sword executes play a particle burst and chime and count the weapon that dealt the hit.

## Changed

- Added in-game configuration access for independent aurora sky and sound controls. Disabling aurora sounds now mutes music and start/end cues and stops active music.

- Shimmerweave Skates freeze water while standing still and no longer lose durability when turning lava to obsidian.
- Aurora music plays once per aurora on the music channel at half volume. Nautilus sounds use the friendly creatures channel.
- Snow Angels need both hands empty.

## Fixed

- Fixed Shimmering Ice hydrating farmland at the wrong height.
- Fixed Ender Bloom conversion losing the snow stored under an Aurora Bloom.
- Reset aurora rendering intensity when disconnecting.
- Corrected guide descriptions of Nautilus drops and following behavior, and Hot Cocoa nutrition.

- Worn Shimmerweave armor no longer shows the missing texture checkerboard. Thanks to smcnultyos. (breakinblocks/auroral#18)
- Setting `basin_max_aura` above 3 no longer crashes the server once a Glacial Basin fills up. The config is now capped at 3.
- Shimmer Soil no longer turns into dirt when jumped on.
- Shimmersteel Hoe and Shovel Silk Touch now uses the real loot tables, so crops drop normally instead of a single seed.
- Shimmersteel Sword executes now properly place snow at the kill site.
- The Auroral Guidebook no longer crashes the game when GuideME is not installed.
- `/aurora start` refuses to run outside the Overworld or during the day instead of reporting success and immediately ending. `/aurora stop` now cleans up blooms and plays the end sound like a natural end.
- `hearthwood_log_burn_time`, `hearthwood_log_frostbite_radius` and `hearthwood_log_villager_discount` are now read. `sword_slowness_duration` is renamed to `sword_frostbite_duration` and controls the Frostbite the sword applies.
- Villagers near a lit Hearthwood Log now give the trade discount.
- Hoppers can no longer jam the Cold Brewing Stand with non-ingredients or overfill bottle slots.
- performance improvements for Hearthwood Logs at nightfall
- Aurora Lanterns placed on the floor under a ceiling no longer hang from the ceiling.
- Aurora gear repair no longer repairs the held item twice per tick.
