# Auroral 1.5.6

## Changed

- Added in-game configuration access for independent aurora sky and sound controls.
- Extended the aurora sound toggle to mute start/end sounds and stop active music when disabled.

## Fixed

- Fixed Cold Brewing Stand inventories disappearing after reload and Glacial Basin contents being lost on non-player destruction.
- Fixed ore duplication with Silk Touch and excessive Fortune drops from Shimmersteel Pickaxes.
- Fixed Hot Cocoa duplicating buckets and failing to restore hunger.
- Fixed material repairs for Shimmersteel tools and Shimmerweave armor.
- Fixed Shimmersteel Bow enchanting, repairs, draw animations, and Flame and Punch effects.
- Fixed Star-Shots losing their weapon enchantments and elapsed lifetime after reload.
- Fixed projectiles gaining Execute when switching to a Shimmersteel Sword before impact.
- Fixed aurora duration-setting crashes and effects lingering after an event ends or a player leaves an eligible biome.
- Fixed Shimmering Ice hydrating farmland at the wrong height.
- Fixed snowlogged blooms losing snow layers, including after Ender Bloom conversion.
- Fixed missing Shimmersteel armor trims and trimmed item icons.
- Fixed Nautili in Stay mode following their owner between dimensions.
- Corrected guide descriptions of Hot Cocoa, Frostbite, and Nautilus drops and following behavior.
