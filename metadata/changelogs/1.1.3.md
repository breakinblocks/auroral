# Auroral 1.1.3

## Added

- Added an `aurora_dimensions` server config option for choosing which dimensions can have auroras. It defaults to the Overworld only. (breakinblocks/auroral#20)

## Fixed

- Fixed tamed Nautili disappearing when following their owner through a Nether portal.
- Fixed tamed Nautili going back through the portal after following their owner to another dimension.
- Fixed tamed Nautili drifting away when their owner is in another dimension or offline. They now hover in place until the owner returns.
