# <Color id="gold">Shimmer Spear</Color>

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  <ItemImage id="shimmer_spear" scale="2" />

  A long-reach piercing spear that rewards committed strikes. Every hit lands Frostbite. During an aurora, every hit lands harder.
</Column>

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>

<RecipeFor id="shimmer_spear_smithing" />

Created by upgrading an **Iron Spear** at a Smithing Table with a **Shimmersteel Upgrade Template** and a <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_ingot" />.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Spear Combat</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmer_spear" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Reach &amp; Charge</Color>
</Row>

* Extended reach for striking from further away.
* Piercing damage interacts with shields differently from a sword.
* Right-click to charge a stab; release for a committed strike.
* Minimum attack charge of 100% — full-cooldown attacks hit hardest.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Frostbite on Hit</Color>
</Column>

Every landing strike applies Frostbite to the target. Frostbite slows movement, reduces healing and armor, and makes shields wear faster. Further hits refresh the effect.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Aurora Enhancement</Color>
</Column>

* During auroras, each stab deals additional damage.
* Aurora-empowered Frostbite lasts twice as long at a higher strength.
* Aurora particles trail from impact hits.

<ItemImage id="minecraft:air" scale="0.25"/>
***

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Tips</Color>
</Column>

* Use the reach to poke through crowds and fences without exposing yourself.
* Time a full-charge stab as enemies close in — partial swings hit softer.
* Pairs well with <ItemLink id="shimmerweave_skates" /> for kiting on ice.
* An aurora overhead is a great window to commit to tougher fights.
