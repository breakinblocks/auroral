<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Auroral Nautilus</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Wild Behavior</Color>
</Row>

A peaceful floating creature that only appears during auroras, drifting through the sky with a softly glowing shell.

* Spawns only during active auroras
* Floats gently in circular patterns
* Peaceful and non-hostile
* Comes in three sizes
* Fades away gracefully 30 seconds after the aurora ends

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aurora_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Attracting Nautili</Color>
</Row>

Wild Nautili are curious and drift toward players holding an <ItemLink id="aurora_shard" />.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aurora_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Taming</Color>
</Row>

Feed a wild Nautilus an Aurora Shard. Each shard has a **33% chance** to tame. Once tamed:

* The nautilus will orbit slowly around you.
* It no longer despawns when the aurora ends.
* Heart particles confirm the bond.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Commanding</Color>
</Row>

* **Shift + Right-click** — toggle between **Follow** (orbits you) and **Stay** (hovers in place).
* Nearby Nautili in Follow mode travel with you through portals and teleports. Nautili in Stay mode remain where you left them.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_nautilus_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Riding</Color>
</Row>

Tamed Nautili can be ridden without a saddle.

1. Right-click to mount (owner only, nautilus must not be in Stay mode).
2. Control flight with movement keys and look direction.
3. Hold **Space** to charge, release for a speed boost up to **3x** when fully charged.

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="shimmersteel_nautilus_armor" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Nautilus Armor</Color>
</Row>

A tamed Auroral Nautilus can wear <ItemLink id="shimmersteel_nautilus_armor" />. Right-click the nautilus while holding the armor to equip it. Right-click with **shears** (without sneaking) to take it off again. The armor drops when the nautilus dies.

<RecipeFor id="shimmersteel_nautilus_armor" />

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="aurora_shard" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Drops</Color>
</Row>

When a player kills a Nautilus, it may drop:

* 1–2 Leather
* A Prismarine Shard (5% chance)
* Nautilus Shells (15% chance, player kills only)

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="roasted_snowball" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">Offending the Nautili</Color>
</Row>

Eating a <ItemLink id="roasted_snowball" /> offends the Aurora. While you carry that offense, wild Nautili **will not spawn near you** during auroras. Only tamed Nautili remain. To atone, craft and eat a <ItemLink id="sugared_roasted_snowball" /> and the skies will grace you again.

<Column alignItems="center" fullWidth={true}>
  ## <Color id="gold">Auroral Snowlette</Color>
</Column>

<Row>
  <ItemImage id="auroral_snowlette_spawn_egg" />
  ### <Color id="aqua">A Pocket-Sized Snow Golem</Color>
</Row>

A small, friendly companion created by right-clicking a Snow Golem with an <ItemLink id="aurora_shard" />.

* Follows the player like a pet
* About 1/4 the size of a normal Snow Golem
* Heals when you throw snowballs at it
* Emits gentle aurora sparkles
